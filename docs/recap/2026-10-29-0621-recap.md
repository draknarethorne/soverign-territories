# Retrospective: 2026-10-28 — intake throughput, matching and storage evidence

**Retrospective day:** 2026-10-28  
**Written:** 2026-10-29, 06:21 -05:00 (delayed; not a contemporaneous log)

## The day in a few lines

Parallel preparation was measured at full scale, a matching-input defect was
reproduced and corrected, and a post-reboot storage audit quantified the
generated Working footprint. These results improved the evidence base, but
they did not accept every output, install the matching preview, settle run
retention or recreate Working. The remaining intake and cast gates are in the
[intake plan](../plans/2026-10-23-image-intake-preparation.md), the
[cast-rebase plan](../plans/2026-10-23-cast-seeded-library-rebase.md), the
[performance plan](../plans/2026-10-28-intake-performance.md), and the
[storage plan](../plans/2026-10-28-storage-operations-and-retention.md).

## What was good

- Whole-image process-pool preparation, worker/thread limits and PNG
  compression changes were tested against deterministic output. Three workers
  were the practical default; four did not improve throughput. The
  full `r21b-20261028` run covered all 2,741 outcomes in 2 h 40 m 37 s
  (17.1 outcomes/minute), compared with r20's 5 h 02 m (about 9.1/minute):
  approximately 1.9x throughput. The outcome buckets were unchanged.
- The run still reported distinct recommendations: 2,372 original,
  222 head-overlay reconstructions, 104 composition, 32 first-crop and 11
  none. The 10-image shift from original to head-overlay reconstruction was
  expected from the new Dola AI reference, not a silent threshold change.
- A replan found that quality checks were reading original pixels while using
  coordinates from the prepared image. A regression reproduced the mismatch.
  After the fix, Review destinations went from 158 to 89, including zero stale
  app-bar holds. This is a corrected proposal, not a movement/install.
- Exact-face evidence was hash-checked for 2,448 artifacts. One measured cache
  entry had no persisted face; no substitute crop was invented. The
  post-reboot audit also distinguished canonical sources from generated run
  copies and removed only a verified orphan staging directory.
- The fixed 112x112 SFace alignment was retained. A wider chin/context view is
  a separate human-review aid (T3), not a change to the pixels used for
  likeness scores.

## What was bad

The full run remained expensive despite the speedup. Matching wall-clock time
was not recorded in the reviewed evidence and is not inferred here. The
89-item Review queue still includes small-group resemblance, same-person
ambiguity, ambiguous cast matches, missing IDs and face-quality/face-count
holds. All 13 cast releases were from Angelica; destination-cluster and
protected-core comparisons still need acceptance. Five representative
residual/partial GMW or caption issues were named in the plans, and the
contact-sheet screen of 106 head-overlay cases was not a full-resolution audit
of all 436 changed recommendations.

The storage scan showed how many generated files make inventory expensive.
That is a real operational cost, but it does not justify deleting historical
runs before their references and retention policy are understood. The
measured duplicate bytes are not a deletion or deduplication authorization.

## What could be improved

- Continue the automated per-row audit before asking for visual review. Report
  changed recommendations, exact face/source bindings, uncertain match
  reasons and destinations; reserve human review for identity truth,
  synthetic artifacts and true policy choices (I59-I60, C7-C9).
- Record elapsed time separately for preparation and matching on future runs.
  The preparation timing is strong; absent matching timing must remain
  explicitly unknown.
- Use a dependency-aware retention policy with a preview/check and deliberate
  apply. The plan's candidate rule is still awaiting the user's keep decision
  (T4/L20); do not prune based on age or apparent duplication.
- Keep performance tests and storage measurements scoped: the B: comparisons
  were single runs, and repeated recursive metadata walks were slower than a
  simple bulk-throughput comparison would suggest.

## Recurring themes

This continues the prior pattern: exact provenance and automated checks
prevented silent mismatches, while image quality and identity remained
human-review questions. Throughput improvements matter only when they preserve
the same inputs, ordering and results. A lower Review count is not inherently
better if it is produced by forcing assignments or weakening thresholds; no
threshold was lowered here.

## Complexity and debt

The post-reboot, no-follow inventory counted about 135.5 GiB across generated
Working roots and 266,337 files in 29,866 directories. Four full intake runs
accounted for about 101.97 GiB and 174,569 files. Recursive inventory passes
on large trees took roughly 34-56 seconds per root. The retention, writer/
reader dependency and storage-policy work remains open (S1, S4-S5, S10-S11,
S14-S16 and L20); these measurements are not permission to remove anything.
