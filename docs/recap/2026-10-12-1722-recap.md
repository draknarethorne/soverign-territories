## What was bad

- **Wrong half of the problem first.** The first reset only matched photos to people who already existed and never grouped the rest, so 32 photos were left in Fantasy's gallery. You had to ask twice. I judged the result by "nothing crosses" instead of by your stated goal, "every good photo gets a hero". A one-line count of what was left, by reason, would have shown it at once (see `likeness status` in the tooling plan).
- **The prospects question was answered late.** "Are prospects compared with the angels and sisters?" had a real answer in the code: `sort` did, `rematch` and `singles` only listed. The look-alike guard, a deliberate earlier design, made the tools stop short of merging, and nobody had written down that this was a choice.
- **Hidden failure in the todo generator.** `docs/todo.md` silently showed 19 of 38 open boxes because it read only one of two `Status:` formats. Nothing complained. The queue you rely on was half empty for days.
- **Hung processes cost about 40 minutes.** Python processes were suspended by Windows (all threads "Suspended") and looked hung. I first suspected my own code, then killed one mid-save and left a truncated JPEG. Atomic writes (a temporary name, then a rename) should have been the rule from the first crop.
- **Line endings and quoting.** One rewrite turned a 2,000-line CRLF file into LF; PowerShell's `Set-Content` added a BOM; heredocs and nested quotes broke several commands. Each cost a turn or two. The edit tool and byte-level helpers are the safe way; the repo mixes CRLF and LF and git warns on every command.
- **Duplicated lists.** STATUS.md listed the plans and a roadmap, the plans README listed the plans, `todo.md` listed boxes. The same fact in three places is how the queue went stale. Fixed today: the plans README is the one index, `todo.md` the one queue, the roadmap moved into a plan.
- **Notes lingering.** Eleven notes sat in `tmp/` for up to four days, some with ideas that existed nowhere else (the identity-LoRA idea, the prompt standards). Cleared today, and now part of the day's hygiene.
- **Crop log gaps.** Two crop runs that I had to kill never wrote their log, so `--undo` did not know 56 of the crops. Reconciled from the archive tonight, and `crop` now saves the log after every photo (the other tools still need the same, D6 in the tooling plan).
