## What was good

- Evidence before claims. The Oct 24 "is it really verified?" question changed the habit: "rendered, not selected" and
  "completed, not accepted" are now said plainly.
- Live runs found what unit tests missed:
  - an empty aborted run folder that made every Review photo unverified;
  - a race that could erase a prompt id;
  - cast-output collisions;
  - the PNG `MemoryError`.
- Every step is a previewable launcher command (`--dry-run`, then `--apply`), and receipts make reruns resume rather than resubmit.
- The queue is shared politely: the runner waits for your own jobs and never cancels or reorders them.
