# Prompt migration: omissions and adaptations for review

This is a **working review ledger**, not a complete clause-by-clause audit.
It records known material differences and identifies sources still needing
comparison. Names below identify the saved manual workflows; their hashes and
the current mapping are in the [workflow plan](../plans/2026-10-22-manual-prompts-to-art-library.md#nov-24-source-comparison-and-integrated-workflow-work).
The manual sources remain unchanged. No generated prompt, workflow, render
or visual result is implied by an assembly being fixture-tested.

The user wants useful intent preserved with non-explicit wording where
possible. The adaptations below are attempts, not claims of equivalence.
Clothing-removal intermediates and sexualized anatomical instructions are
not carried into the pipeline. This ledger summarizes categories rather than
reproducing source wording. A missing row or an open comparison does **not**
mean nothing was omitted.

For male Angel repository auditions, the female `Background_Bust` selection
is not copied. A separately named `Background_Frame` selects the character's
male frame instead; it is not an anatomical equivalent. The two Plus
adaptations retain the source-color two-piece swimsuit and two Deluxe
adaptations retain heels as explicit audition choices, not default wardrobe.
Review these choices and all results; no male auditions have been rendered.

Coverage is not yet available for every assigned character. The ten Bound
identities have no configured selected photos, their home workspace is planned
and their group is skipped by workflow commands. They do not have the new
character-specific Edit suite; do not interpret the Angel and Sister
repository auditions as Bound coverage. The Sister and male Angel auditions
remain repository-only, not installed or visually accepted. Source selection,
workspace publication and the planned-home transition remain separate gates,
owned by the
[workflow plan](../plans/2026-10-22-manual-prompts-to-art-library.md#nov-24-source-comparison-and-integrated-workflow-work).
