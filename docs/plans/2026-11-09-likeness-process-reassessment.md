## End goals and operating principles - Nov 13 agreement

**Agreed Nov 13 after follow-up Q&A.** This is the front-door summary of the
existing Q&A, not a new execution queue. The user selects repair, iterative
outcome audit, then design/configuration simplification evaluation.
The clarified 0.80 starting-backbone rule is distinct from later admission/
reassignment gates and is not a permanent placement lock. Exact feedback lives in
[the scope-test source record](2026-11-12-member-refinement-scope-test.md#nov-13-1009-goals-first-repair-and-simplification-request).
The Nov 13 audit remains evidence that the current proposal is incomplete.

1. **One coherent identity library, not just better scores.** Starting from
   the approved existing organisation plus incoming Various photos, recover
   who belongs together, preserve correct existing identities and finish
   with named, browsable outcomes and explicit unresolved exceptions.
2. **Routine intake handles new photos and better versions.** Distinguish
   exact duplicates, same-photo versions and merely similar photos. Clean
   incoming images, select a genuinely preferable usable version with
   original/version lineage and recovery, then place it with its rightful
   identity. Newer alone is not better.
3. **Recover real members upwards.** Match arrivals and existing misplaced
   photos across the whole identity universe. After same-person evidence
   qualifies, keep the higher-ranked canonical owner:
   Sisters > Angels > Models > Prospects > Potentials. Rank never proves
   identity. Distinct authored cast slots remain separate.
4. **Preserve established high-confidence identity continuity.** A rebase
   should not erase a sound identity and demand it rediscover itself from
   nothing. Retain a verified strong baseline reference backbone, reassess
   weaker/core-excluded members, and allow additional independently qualified
   appearance views. Losing reference support alone does not eject members.
   Do not silently restore genuinely disqualified references to manufacture
   continuity.
5. **Correct real mistakes, without forcing borderline moves.** Obvious
   wrong-person members may leave even a cast identity when independent
   destination and source-correction evidence qualify and hard locks permit.
   Borderline or reference-insufficient existing identity members stay
   physically in place with visible uncertainty; failed entrance alone is
   not an eviction rule. Various intake is different: resolve its usable
   members into safe assignments or provisional Potentials, not permanent
   intake holds merely because they cannot qualify as references.
6. **Complete regrouping has three distinct outcomes.** Qualifying clusters
   become supported same-person appearances, named tentative associations,
   or genuine named independent identities. This works throughout the
   category ladder, not only for cast parents. Qualifying new clusters must
   not remain in Various simply because no existing parent matched.
   Assignment and reference qualification are separate: usable unmatched
   cohorts, including singletons, may establish provisional non-core
   Potentials without claiming confirmed identity or voting authority.
   Quality-held/ambiguous exceptions remain explicit rather than forced.
7. **Membership, reference authority and physical intent stay separate.**
   Supported Gallery membership need not imply core use. Tentative members
   do not certify themselves or neighbours. Keep, hard placement locks,
   workflow choices and group-name/parent protection retain their different
   meanings. Fictional family naming/default roles have separate gates;
   they are neither proof of identity nor biological-family inference.
8. **Prove the outcome in one maintained, repeatable process.** Ordinary
   intake and deliberate rebase should use the same decision machinery with
   explicit scope, not divergent engines. Preserve one physical home and
   lineage. Show actual stage changes, reference evidence, rivals, holds,
   new identities, cast gains/losses and replacement decisions in usable
   bounded viewers. Component/integrity checks do not replace behavioral,
   held-out and human acceptance. Preview approval precedes installation;
   Production remains separately gated.

### Strong Gallery members: agreed starting-backbone intent

The user clarifies **0.80 or higher against its own identity** as the
initial Angels/Sisters reference-backbone selection at the start of rebase,
not the gate for new arrivals or later reassignment. Preserve that strong
starting evidence rather than make full rebase synonymous with total reset.
All other photos remain free to reassess under the separate agreed gates.
This does not hard-lock backbone members: automatic correction is allowed
when independent evidence very clearly proves another identity.

Before implementing an automatic rule, establish which recorded score is
meant: scoring model/normalisation, own-reference inventory, content binding,
quality and independence (no self/duplicate vote), and measurement date.
Similarity is not a calibrated probability. A score against a fragmented or
contaminated reference set cannot certify its own correctness merely by
being high.

**Implementation interpretation:** preserve verified strong
existing same-person Gallery members as the baseline reference backbone,
including qualifying 0.80+ members under the agreed score definition.
Missing or incomparable score evidence creates a visible verification hold,
not automatic eviction or a manufactured zero. Retain the identity while
qualified coherent views are reconstructed. Separate duplicate/nonindependent
membership from reference voting. New contradictory quality, content-binding
or identity evidence must be explicit in the decision record. Very clear
independently qualified wrong-identity correction may move even a strong
baseline member, subject to the normal hard-lock and source/destination gates.
Missing reference support or a slightly stronger rival alone cannot do so.
Explicit locks remain a distinct control. Baseline seed selection, membership
admission, reference growth and correction thresholds must have different
names/provenance in the receipt even when numerical values happen to agree.

### Simplification direction to evaluate during repair

The intended outcomes are coherent, but the current implementation and
overlapping policy layers are not yet a demonstrated solid end-to-end design.
The audit found missing lifecycle operations and inconsistent state export,
not simply thresholds that need relaxing. Raising or lowering one threshold
cannot repair those structural defects.

Prefer **three operational stages** over additional independent pipelines:

1. **Prepare and establish baseline:** inventory/version cleanup and
   content-bound measurements; preserve verified identity backbones and
   reconstruct all existing identity hypotheses. Reuse prepared inputs.
2. **Resolve identities once:** one authoritative reference ledger and one
   shared policy decision for retain, supported association, tentative hold,
   strong correction or independent identity. Reconcile cross-folder
   hypotheses and bounded validated growth inside this stage; do not make
   the user run separate matching/refinement/regroup commands.
3. **Review and publish:** actual immutable before/after boundary snapshots,
   one compact exception/change ledger and bounded viewers; install only the
   approved proposal with recovery and consumer verification.

Fewer user-facing stages do not remove independent evidence, rival, quality,
reference-use or protection safeguards. Consolidate conflicting threshold
sources and duplicated state computations instead. Distinguish missing
implementation, policy-disabled actions, insufficient data and unaccepted
accuracy. Keep readiness classification, secondary-cue experiments, fictional
role automation and live GUI actions explicitly owned/deferred rather than
quietly making every one a prerequisite for the bounded rebase repair.

## Series evidence and safeguards

Use distinctive outfit details/patterns and garment shape rather than colour
alone. Scene, lighting and reliable capture metadata may corroborate a
series; missing metadata must stay missing. Generated or altered series may
reuse clothes/backgrounds across different faces and must be counterexamples.

Haircut and styling may also corroborate a series: length, fringe/parting,
texture and updo shape, with colour treated cautiously under changing light.
They are supporting appearance cues, not independent identity anchors.
Occlusion, cropping and pose can hide hair; missing evidence is not a mismatch.
The same person can change styling within a series, and different people can
share a hairstyle or wig. Neither agreement nor disagreement alone assigns
or splits identity, or overrides stronger face evidence.

- Preserve explicit manual protection, anchors, opt-outs and rejections.
- Preserve quality, independent support, competing-identity evidence and
  content binding. No automatic gate relaxation is approved here.
- A stronger contradictory face match cannot be overridden solely by clothing.
- Do not merge entire identities because a few members share an outfit.
- Do not let an outfit bridge propagate transitively into unrelated outfits.
- Do not count near-duplicate photos from one session as independent anchors.
