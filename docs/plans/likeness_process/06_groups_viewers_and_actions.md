| Control | Intended semantics |
| --- | --- |
| Accept recommended association/move | Explicit scoped decision with current source/evidence validation, per-member exceptions, visible result/history and reversible action |

| Protect group name/parent relationship | Preserve organisational decision and stable lineage, not automatic per-member Keep/locks or matching-reference eligibility |

membership. Accepting the parent association is separate. Members remain
individually assessable; a protected group can shrink while its historical
record survives. Existing protection records must not silently become
overrideable Keep. No new fields, migration or automatic override of old hard
locks is approved by a shadow report.
