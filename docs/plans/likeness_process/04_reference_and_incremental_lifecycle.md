## Outcome and delivered reuse

### Nov 12 directed shadow lifecycle

The [Nov 12 answers](../2026-11-12-member-refinement-scope-test.md#nov-12-directed-reformation-qa)
select validated appearance-reference recovery under RA2b's bounded shadow
comparison. Unlike automatic same-pass recursion, an appearance first needs
independently qualified members, then an explicit frozen reference-validation
boundary before supporting later members. Tentative members never contribute
reference authority. Keep exact prerequisite witnesses, parent evidence,
content/near-duplicate exclusions and distinct-rival checks; do not launder a
new member into independent support through a chain of subsequent additions.

Freeze strong initial-core scores and evidence context for continuity
diagnostics. Reassessed reference qualification can withdraw influence
without changing physical placement. Insufficient references pause dependent
automatic decisions; they do not prove wrong membership or justify filling a
core with weak members. Lost prerequisite support invalidates dependent
reference additions. Bound transitions explicitly and report incomplete scope
rather than claim convergence at a resource cap.

Compare no-growth and staged-growth behavior on identical saved measurements.
The existing one-growth-boundary runtime is historical control, not proof
this wider recovery contract is delivered. Hard placement locks and
overrideable Keep are distinct under phase06; legacy protection records keep
their existing meaning.

### Nov 10 bounded frozen-boundary continuation

The opt-in membership-assessment builder now continues reference
withdrawals through at most four frozen boundaries rather than leaving a
final reference/member veto as an unexplained pending state.
Each boundary records the inventory count, exact qualification/admission
withdrawals and remaining pending views. Reassess all scoped members and
competitors after a withdrawal; no same-pass reference growth, tentative
promotion or erased rival hypothesis is permitted.

The original discovery pool/groups stay fixed during this continuation.
Reference IDs must monotonically shrink and view IDs must remain identical;
an unexpected new view or reintroduced reference fails explicitly.
This is deliberately not the growth/incremental/full rediscovery stage.
Newly released old references remain independently visible residuals until
a separately constructed discovery boundary. Reaching a fixed point in
this bounded inventory does not establish correct identity assignments or
complete a rebase. Tests cover fixed pool, exact transition history,
no-growth failure, changed-competitor failure and capped incompleteness.

Saved-population suffix05 reaches this bounded fixed point in two
boundaries: 681 qualified references, then 16 final-veto withdrawals and
three further qualification withdrawals, leaving 662. Zero pending views;
all 3,290 photos remain represented. The action outcomes are unchanged
from suffix03: consistency is repaired, known splits are not declared
recovered. Forward/reverse/publication takes 160.143s.

## Implementation sequence

### Nov 11 selected whole-population runtime continuation

RA2h now integrates qualification, member reassessment, withdrawal and one
original-witness growth boundary into the opt-in maintained rebase. The first
real 3,290-photo preview exposed a genuine integration limit: its inherited
four-boundary shadow cap stopped before reference/member consistency settled.
This is not evidence that the remaining photos belong elsewhere.

The whole-population runtime uses a finite monotonic fixed point instead:
every changing boundary strictly removes reference occurrences, so at most the
starting occurrence count plus one assessment is necessary. It records exact
withdrawals, phase, boundary and remaining references. No threshold is lowered,
competitor hidden or uncertainty certified to make it terminate. Initial
reference qualification has the same strict-decrease guarantee. The older
four-pass experimental contract above remains historical evidence.

Growth is one separately recorded addition boundary, never part of that
withdraw-only solver. Every addition retains its exact original witnesses;
the post-growth assessment withdraws it if fewer than four independent
surviving original witnesses remain. Additions cannot certify each other.
Membership, margins and reference authority are reassessed after growth.
Withdrawal pages contain the affected references, explicitly scoped against
the full population; milestone pages retain complete population snapshots.

Exact leave-one-out scoring excludes every equal-content copy. Frozen
reference sums are reused without caching member decisions. Focused
verification now passes 109 tests, including exact centroid-oracle checks,
post-growth reassessment and FAST/LEAN/FULL synthetic publication/check/undo.
The actual selected FAST preview/install is still in progress; these tests
do not establish blind clustering accuracy or dependency-scoped daily parity.

The real population now reaches consistency in seven membership boundaries:
1,311 reference occurrences fall to 1,121, with the seventh boundary making
no withdrawal. One staged growth boundary adds 1,321 occurrences; three
post-growth assessments settle at 2,397 occurrences. Occurrences are not
unique photos: one photo can witness several views of the same parent.
These measured transitions explain why four passes were insufficient.
The later publication blockers are family-graph reconciliation (05) and
bounded lossless receipt serialization (07), not a new scoring threshold.

1. Separate placement intent, reference use, automatic stability, art
   selection and character-slot protection. Preserve existing metadata
   semantics until an explicitly versioned migration/selected scope exists;
   do not reinterpret a saved protected flag as permission to unlock.
2. Give each reference set an evidence/version boundary, independent
   support and sufficiency state. Qualify multi-appearance views rather than
   simply averaging every admitted photo. Selected/non-core/tentative and
   low-quality states must remain distinct.
3. Freeze references per pass. Withdraw unsupported/diluting reference
   influence independently of physical movement. Lack of support is not
   proof of another person; retain a non-core association where appropriate.
   Respect explicit placement restrictions without forcing reference use.
4. Stage qualified automatic growth and explicit manual opt-in separately.
   Validate independent eligible support, rivals and diversity before the
   next boundary. No photo/equal-content/series self-certification, same-pass
   bootstrap, single-best bridge or incidental protection acquisition.
5. If references become insufficient, preserve identity/Gallery/character,
   surface reference-needed and pause only dependent automatic decisions.
   Seek qualified replacement views, not tentative members promoted merely
   to fill a quota or questionable references kept to make automation run.
6. Distinguish ordinary-batch stability from deliberate rebase release.
   Unprotected eligible old members must be reconsiderable; workflow
   selection alone is not target identity truth. Character-slot replacement,
   explicit locks and source scope remain separate permissions.
7. Maintain content/reference/policy/evidence dependencies. On change,
   reassess affected members, appearance groups and competitors including
   old photos. Expand when changed references affect further identities.
   Retain explicit incomplete/escalation status if bounded closure fails.
8. Keep ordinary rejection observations, reasons and evidence versions.
   A materially changed qualified result may reverse them with an explicit
   old/new explanation. Unchanged rerun is not material change; explicit
   placement restrictions remain independently respected. Negative/conflict
   regression evidence cannot be silently cleared.
9. Preserve bounded transition/oscillation history through admission and
   post-admission refinement. Cache unchanged measurements, not stale
