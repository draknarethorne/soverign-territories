### Recommended state contract

| State | Meaning and presentation | Discovery / reference authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person identity | Stable parent identity, character-facing name and original-person lineage | Qualified identity references only; placement/protection/art selection remain separate |
| Primary photos | Supported members not organised into a named appearance; may include non-core photos under Q&A27 | Main placement alone never grants core use |
| Supported named appearance | Coherent, independently supported same-person variation under that parent, with stable group ID/name/member IDs and attach/detach history | Its descriptors can retrieve similar photos; identity association still needs qualified parent/view or validated combined evidence |
| Tentative named appearance | Coherent candidate with a defensible parent association but insufficient identity evidence or unresolved rivals; visibly uncertain under the candidate parent | Can suggest discovery comparisons, not certify new parent membership, contribute core votes or count as accepted readiness |
| Independent resembling identity | Coherent candidate that should remain separate; optionally linked through fictional family presentation | Own stable person/group state, not a nested appearance merely because a same-person gate failed |
| Review work queue | Unresolved parent, mixed membership, contradictory/insufficient evidence, decode errors or an action needing attention | A task/status filter across states, not the physical home of every non-core photo or a substitute for appearance structure |

A group name/ID is first-class tracked state, **not** automatically a second
canonical person. Preserve an attached source person's old ID/name as
lineage so a qualified later detach can restore continuity without creating
duplicate ownership. A wholly new appearance needs its own stable group
identifier/name; it need not invent a separate fictional person first.
Family labels concern distinct or deliberately independent identities and
are fictional UX, not a lower-similarity disposal bucket.

**Low similarity is a reason to assess, not a sufficient appearance rule.**
Use coherent variation, per-member compatibility, qualified same-person
evidence and outside-rival/conflict checks. A low-scoring unrelated or
ambiguous photo must not be hidden beneath a character as an appearance.
A high-scoring variation may still be a useful named group. Separate:

1. query-to-parent identity/view score and qualified witness count;
2. query-to-appearance coherence/nearest members and their independence;
3. full outside-person rivals, exact conflicts and signed margin;
4. supported/tentative membership, core eligibility and permission to act.

Individual admission is allowed when that photo qualifies. Whole-group
attachment is only a batched set of member decisions: split out exceptions
and reassess residuals. A high centroid or a few strong photos cannot carry
weak members into a confirmed identity. Group coherence and parent
association require separate evidence and separate recorded results.

An appearance may help **find** additional matching photos. Promoting it
to a qualified local identity reference view is a later explicit frozen
boundary, with independent support/diversity, parent compatibility and
negative/competitor validation under [04](04_reference_and_incremental_lifecycle.md).
Simply matching a tentative group cannot propagate a supported parent
claim through a chain. The existing face-only non-core-association trial
still applies ordinary entrance gates and yields zero new qualified
non-core placements: it has not implemented this wider appearance route.
No new appearance/family numerical cutoff is selected at this checkpoint.
