image clusters for fictional card-art identities, not discover a real
person's name or establish their biological relationships. Preserve original
identity/character names and reversible lineage as user-facing continuity;
new generated names and sister/mother/etc. labels supply fictional history.

features or grounds to force incompatible photos together.

invent real-world identity facts, infer biological family, or abandon
member/reference/appearance safeguards. Do not equate an art-useful

assessing who belongs together. Names, category and family tree must not

lineage when supported fragments consolidate or later detach.

Existing code supplies categories, continuity, surname/family links and
reversible metadata infrastructure. That does not implement the Q&A's new
readiness meaning or automatic same-person/appearance/distinct-person
