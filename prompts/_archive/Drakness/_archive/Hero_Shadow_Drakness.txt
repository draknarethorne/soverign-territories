=== HERO — Drakness, the Shadow Necromancer (Dark/Shadow element) ===
Subject: A seductive young necromancer sorceress, confident and commanding, mid-cast.
Element & palette: Shadow/Arcane — dominant deep purple with magenta glow and drifting black smoke.
Outfit: an elegant layered ethereal gown of frosted bright purple over a matte underlayer, delicate glowing purple fiber-optic threads woven throughout; secured at the shoulders, head uncovered.
Accents: silver with purple-jeweled bracelets and necklace.
Weapon: an ornate staff raised in her left hand, a skull-shaped purple jewel at the top wreathed in bright purple glow.
Spell / pose: right arm bent at the elbow, hand cupped, fusing a ball of purple fumes and dark smoke as she casts.
Environment: a castle dungeon wreathed in swirling purple and black smoke, a soft purple gradient haze rising behind her.
Footwear: bright purple high heels with glowing purple straps.
