Identity lock: the face in the reference photo is the face of the result, copied exactly and never replaced by an invented or idealised one: the same bone structure, eye shape and spacing, brows, nose, lips, jawline and skin. If the head in the reference is turned, tilted or seen at an angle, turn it to face the camera squarely as the same person photographed straight on, keeping those same features.

Framing: show her from the top of the head to the feet, standing, the whole body in the picture; where the reference shows only her head and upper body, continue the figure downward to the feet.
