## 🎯 Phase 2 Features

### Economy System
- **AFK Production**: Workers generate Food/Lumber/Ore passively (30/hour, 12-hour cap)
- **Building Placement**: Territory tiles have 5-15 building slots (Castle Level-gated)
- **Resource Costs**: Battles cost 20 Food, buildings cost Lumber/Ore
- **Crafting**: Equipment sockets + Jewels/Runes for stat customization

### New Card Types (Planned)
- **Element Buildings**: Fire Foundry, Water Mill, Earth Quarry (3-6 per element)
- **Legendary Equipment**: High-rarity gear with unique effects
- **Neutral Heroes**: 2-4 heroes for hybrid elemental decks

## 🔍 Design Notes

**Why Defer Economy Cards?**
- **MVP Focus**: Core loop is collect→battle→win, not manage economy
- **Complexity Budget**: Adding 44 economy cards + placement systems dilutes battle gameplay
- **Progression Arc**: Phase 2 introduces resource scarcity, making economy meaningful
- **Art Pipeline**: Buildings/equipment require unique art (not just palette swaps)

**Base Set Without Economy**:
- 15 Heroes, 104 Units, 12 Tactics = **131 battle cards**
- Still achieves 150+ card threshold for collector appeal
- Pure battle focus = clearer MVP messaging ("TCG meets tactics")
